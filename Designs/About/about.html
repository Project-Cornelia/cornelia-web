The Journey

From Student Vision to Institutional Impact

What began as a passionate student-run club at Lady Shri Ram College has evolved into a formidable independent organization under the AINA Foundation. Our journey has been defined by the pursuit of making intellectual discourse accessible to those often left out of the room.

To date, we have spearheaded over 30+ awareness activities across diverse socio-economic landscapes. Our initiatives have directly touched the lives of more than 300+ females, providing them with the tools of democratic literacy and civic agency.

Our signature project, 'The Constitution Land', stands as a testament to our editorial approach—simplifying complex constitutional frameworks into engaging, accessible narratives for young learners and grassroot leaders alike.
